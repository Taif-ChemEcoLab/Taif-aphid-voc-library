# Data licensing and attribution

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies only to original VOC·BIO application code. It does not grant rights in scientific data or third-party content.

The files under `data/v1/`, `data/docking/`, and `docking_v1/` combine curated project records with information derived from or linked to published literature, RCSB PDB, PubChem, and other external sources. No blanket CC BY 4.0 license is applied to these directories. Each source component remains subject to its original rights, terms, and attribution requirements.

The primary published source for the aphid headspace GC-MS evidence is:

Alotaibi, N. J., Alsufyani, T., M’sakni, N. H., Almalki, M. A., Alghamdi, E. M., & Spiteller, D. (2023). “Rapid Identification of Aphid Species by Headspace GC-MS and Discriminant Analysis.” *Insects*, 14, 589. https://doi.org/10.3390/insects14070589

RCSB PDB structures, PubChem records and images, published annotations, external database records, and third-party software are not relicensed by this repository. Users are responsible for consulting and complying with the applicable source terms. Provenance fields and source citations must be retained when reusing curated records.
